CNN Fear & Greed Index — Candlestick Chart

The 1M, 3M and 6M views plot daily candles: high and low are the day’s tracked intraday extremes (5-minute samples, from Mon 2026-11-02), close is CNN’s 16:00 ET close, and the open is the day’s first tracked sample (recorded from 2026-11-25; earlier tracked days use the previous close clamped into the day’s range). Days before tracking began have no tracked extremes, so their candles run from the previous close to the close, with no wick beyond the body. The 1Y and All views plot weekly candles built from daily closes (open = the week’s first close, high/low = its highest/lowest closes, close = its last close): CNN publishes daily scores only, with no historical opens or intraday extremes, so this is the honest long-frame form.
