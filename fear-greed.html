CNN Fear & Greed Index — Candlestick Chart

The 1M, 3M and 6M views plot daily candles: high and low are the day’s tracked intraday extremes (5-minute samples, from Mon 2026-11-02), close is CNN’s 16:00 ET close, and the open is the day’s first tracked sample (recorded from 2026-11-25; earlier tracked days use the previous close clamped into the day’s range). Days before tracking began have no tracked extremes, so their candles run from the previous close to the close, with no wick beyond the body. The 1Y and All views plot weekly candles built from daily closes (open = the week’s first close, high/low = its highest/lowest closes, close = its last close): CNN publishes daily scores only, with no historical opens or intraday extremes, so this is the honest long-frame form. The 1W view plots 30-minute candles built from the tracker’s 5-minute samples for the trailing 7 days (sample collection began Fri 2026-11-27); a day with no samples recorded yet shows its daily candle — the same candle the 1M view plots for that day — in the 3:30pm slot. The 1D view plots the most recent session’s 5-minute samples as a line — a tick chart, one dot per sample across the 9:30–16:00 ET session, no candles. In the 1M, 1W and 1D views an S&P 500 overlay sits on its own right-hand axis — daily OHLC bars in the 1M, a line of 30-minute closes in the 1W, a line of 5-minute closes in the 1D — toggled by the SPX button. The 1M bars are coloured like the candles — teal when SPX closed up on the day, red when it closed down, amber only when exactly flat; the 1W and 1D lines stay amber. History from 2025-10-30.

Teal candle: close above the open. Red: below it. Grey: unchanged. Hover any candle for its values. In the 1M view, a tracked day’s high is labelled above its wick and its low below it; the close is unlabelled — where the body ends against the wick is the information. In the 1W view, each day’s high and low are labelled instead of each bar’s. Days are spaced evenly — weekends and holidays take no space. In the 1D view, the line joins the session’s 5-minute samples and the day’s high and low samples are labelled; hover any dot for its time and value. The overlay in the 1M, 1W and 1D views is the S&P 500 (SPX) on its own right-hand axis — daily OHLC bars in the 1M, lifted clear of the candles by the SPX scale’s floor at about 6,600 (stem = the day’s range, left tick = open, right tick = close; the stem’s span between the open and the close is coloured teal when SPX closed up on the day and red when it closed down — amber only when exactly flat — and the ticks and the rest of the stem are grey, like the candles’ wicks), and an amber line in the 1W and 1D; hover it for values, and use the SPX button to show or hide it.
